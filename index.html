01 / THE ARTISTNORTH AFRICA ↔ FRANCE

A meeting point between deep-rooted rhythm and the dance floor.

From Morocco and based in France, Weeyzz (MA) is a Moroccan DJ, electronic music producer and sound engineer exploring the intersection of contemporary electronic music and diverse musical influences.

Moving between Indie Dance and House, his sound combines hypnotic rhythms, melodic textures and organic elements, creating immersive musical journeys shaped by a wide range of sonic inspirations.

Beyond the dancefloor, his artistic approach is driven by a curiosity for musical heritage, cultural exchange and the reinterpretation of traditional sounds within contemporary electronic contexts. His work explores connections between different musical cultures, bringing together local identities and global club influences.

He is also the founder of SOM Music & Culture, an independent cultural organisation developing artistic projects and collaborations across music and culture, including the Sounds of Morocco initiative.

Through DJ sets, productions and cultural collaborations, Weeyzz seeks to build bridges between musical worlds, connecting heritage and contemporary creation.
